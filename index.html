About Sam

Good health, and leaving you re-energised

Sam is a qualified massage therapist who loves the healing side of her work. She practises from a calm studio at her home in Boondall: bamboo walls, orchids, candlelight, and a garden path to the door.

Her clients range from athletes to locals managing long-term pain. Whatever brings you in, she takes time to understand it before she starts.
